   both at once: displacer only (dsp)         1.037128e+00     -174.6
   ... plus the absorber entering (scram1)    1.036682e+00     -219.2

   THE GATE'S SIGN IS NEGATIVE HERE, AND THE REASON IS GEOMETRIC, NOT A
   CANCELLATION.  The displacer is centred in the core when withdrawn.
   Moving it down 1.25 m GAINS graphite in the bottom 1.25 m -- the
   core's lowest-flux band -- and LOSES it five planes below the
   displacer's top, which is still well inside the high-flux region.
   So the sweep is negative for ANY centre-peaked axial flux, and it
   turns positive only once the bottom is weighted more heavily than
   that interior band.  From the two half-steps the required ratio is
   about 3.19 in flux-squared weighting.
