The loop converges, and the coupling machinery is right: NCR: reads
the thermal-hydraulic state back out of the fuel map, MACINI: and
TRIVAA: rebuild on it, and FLPOW: feeds THM: in turn.  The first four
passes contract geometrically and the eigenvalue follows them down to
single-precision roundoff -- dk reaches exactly zero between passes.

THE TEMPERATURE FIELD DOES NOT CONVERGE, AND IT IS NOT GOING TO.
   The fuel-temperature and coolant-density residuals fall for about
   fifteen passes and then plateau, while the field EXTREMA freeze to
   the thousandth and keff goes bit-stable.  Bit-stable keff means
   bit-stable power, so a converged field would give a residual of
   exactly zero; a plateau means a limit cycle.  The mechanism is in
   the source rather than inferred: K0, the index of the axial node
   where boiling starts, is an INTEGER set by the Saha-Zuber test
   IF(TCALO.GE.TSAT-DTSUB) K0=K (THMH2O.f:383) and reset to 0 at :276,
   and it gates the entire two-phase block at :147.  A node within
   rounding of that threshold switches the branch on and off, moving
   its density by a finite step each pass; DTSUB depends on the heat
   flux, so the switch sits inside the Picard loop.  Relaxation scales
   the plateau -- about 7, 2 and 0.6 K at RELAX 1.0, 0.5, 0.2 -- without
   ever reaching zero, and the eigenvalue is BIT-IDENTICAL at all
   three.  So the loop tests dk and reports the field residuals beside
   it, and the residual plateau is a property of the void correlation
   that A8 will march through, not an unconverged steady state.
