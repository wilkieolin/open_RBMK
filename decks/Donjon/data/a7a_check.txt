   The diameter-matched pitch has 28 % less flow area, so at the same
   inlet velocity it carries 28 % less mass and over-predicts the
   quality.  Its velocity landing on 18.5 m/s is arithmetic, not
   agreement: too little mass through too small an area.

   AND THE PITCH CHOICE IS SETTLED BY THESE TWO ROWS.  The clad
   temperatures barely move between the two fits -- a degree, where
   the hydraulic diameter differs by 39 % -- because at this quality
   the surface is in nucleate boiling, where the wall superheat is set
   by the boiling correlation and not by the single-phase film
   coefficient.  So the quantity the area-matched pitch gets wrong
   does not reach any quantity being compared, while the quantity it
   gets right -- the flow area -- sets the quality outright.  The
   nominal choice was argued from the physics beforehand; this is the
   measurement that confirms it, and it means the bracket costs
   nothing to carry.

   the channel, and it is why HGAP should be set explicitly for A7.

   The quality is untouched by HGAP, as it must be: the gap moves heat
   from fuel to coolant without changing how much arrives.  So the
   quality result and the centreline result are independent, and only
   the centreline is sensitive to the unsourced input.
