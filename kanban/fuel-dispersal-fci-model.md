# Fuel fragmentation and dispersal heat transfer (FCI)

The post-AZ-5 drum record needs ~8–19 t of dispersed fuel transferring heat within a second (PROGRESS (46)).
The model has no path for fragmented fuel to heat the coolant directly. A sourced particle-size / heat-transfer
model, triggered by the sourced enthalpy thresholds, would make the drum record a test of the fuel damage.

**Sources:** PROGRESS (46), (47); [NEA10].

## Acceptance Criteria
- A sourced dispersal/FCI model design (trigger, particle size, transfer time constant), off by default; a decision on whether it is needed once cards 27–29 report.
