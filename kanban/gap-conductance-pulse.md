# Pellet–cladding gap conductance during the pulse

The gap conductance is the deck's constant (2.8 kW/m²/K). In a power pulse pellet expansion closes the gap
and the conductance rises, which sets when the cladding heat flux follows the fuel and so the DNB timing.

**Sources:** PROGRESS (7) (gap conductance), (49).

## Acceptance Criteria
- A sourced gap model (gap width from pellet/cladding expansion, gas + contact conductance), off by default with byte-identical results when off; its effect on dryout timing measured.
